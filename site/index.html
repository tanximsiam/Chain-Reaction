Know the
breaking point

Two atomsCorners have two neighbours, so two atoms set them off.
Three atomsEdges hold three before they burst.
Four atomsMiddle cells take four, one for each side.
  • On your turn, add one atom to an empty cell or one of your own.
  • A full cell bursts: one atom flies to each neighbour, and every cell it lands in becomes yours.
  • Neighbours that fill up burst too, so one move can run right across the board.
  • After your first move, losing every atom knocks you out. The last colour standing wins.

Free for Windows

Windows

One file, ChainReaction-windows.exe. Double-click to play. The exe isn’t code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may stop it: choose More info, then Run anyway.

Linux

Needs OpenGL 3.3. Unpack and run:

tar -xzf ChainReaction-linux.tar.gz
./ChainReaction
